   cause shall be in danger of the judgement" was kept as written, with the

   sister will be subject to judgment"); "without a cause" appears only in some
   older manuscripts and is shown in a footnote. Kept as you wrote it, since the
   book quotes elsewhere in the NIV.


A SMALL NOTE (not a fact change)

   righteous anger here, let me know.
