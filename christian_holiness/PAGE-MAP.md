## Numbering and lettering

Dad's four main questions are numbered 1 to 4 and kept as he numbered them. Under
Q4 (the church's role) he typed sub-points (a) and (b) and continued the letters
(c), (d) by hand in the margin; "teach the benefits of holiness" is rendered as
(e) to keep the run going. The three items on sheet 6 after that (imitate Christ;
peace and holiness; be holy as God is) read as a closing exhortation and are left
unlettered.

## Editorial notes

- The opening paragraph is Dad's handwritten definition from the top of sheet 1,
  typed as written (one grammar tidy: "a Christian ... conforms his life").
- There was no theme line or subtitle written on the sheets. For consistency with
  the other chapters, Dad's own closing heading, "Let us be holy, as God is
  holy" (1 Pet 1:14-16 / Lev 11:44), is used as the chapter subtitle. It can be
  changed or removed. See the corrections file.
- Beside Mark 9:50 and 1 Corinthians 5:6-8 Dad wrote "(read the heading)", a note
  telling the reader the verse supports the stated heading. Those notes-to-self
  are left out of the body; the verses sit under their headings.
- The struck-out subheading "What Holiness Isn't" under Q2 was omitted, as Dad
  struck it out.
