CONSCIENCE - POINTS TO REVIEW (FOR DAD)

This note lists every place where a Bible reference or a detail looked worth a
second look while typing the Conscience chapter (the nine handwritten sheets).
Nothing was changed quietly. Each point says exactly what was done so Dad can
confirm or correct it.


CHANGES MADE (please confirm these are okay)

1. Step VI, the David example. The paper cites "1 Samuel 24:1" for David's heart
   smiting him after he cut off a piece of King Saul's robe. 1 Samuel 24:1 is only
   the opening of the chapter (Saul chasing David). The verse where David's
   conscience actually troubles him is 1 Samuel 24:5 ("David was conscience-
   stricken for having cut off a corner of his robe"). It was set to 1 Samuel 24:5.
   Please confirm.

2. Step VI, the Joseph example. The paper cites "Genesis 50:7-8" for Joseph's
   brothers pleading for mercy after their father's death, fearing Joseph's
   revenge. Genesis 50:7-8 is about the journey to bury Jacob, not the plea for
   mercy. The brothers' plea is Genesis 50:15-18. It was set to Genesis 50:15-18.
   (The earlier reference in the same sentence, Genesis 42:21, is correct and was
   kept.) Please confirm.


A NOTE ON THE QUOTED VERSES

3. Several verses were written on the paper inside quotation marks, or marked

   Acts 23:1. The NIV wording differs slightly from the older wording on the paper
   (for example, Romans 9:1 reads "my conscience confirms it through the Holy
   Spirit" in the NIV). Please give these a final read against a printed NIV.
