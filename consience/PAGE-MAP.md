# Conscience, Page Map (9 images, all handwritten English)

All nine sheets are handwritten in English prose, headed "CONSCIENCE". The chapter
is organised by STEP headings (Steps I to VI) plus a closing section, "The three
pictures of conscience", rather than numbered questions. There is no typed Twi
appendix. Verses that Dad marked to be quoted, or wrote out in quotation marks,
are given in the NIV.

Sheets 1 and 2 were photographed upright. Sheets 3 to 8 were photographed
upside-down and were rotated 180 degrees and enhanced for reading. One sheet (the
"Quote" sheet) is the Hamilton quotation.
