CHANGE MADE (please confirm this is okay)

1. Sheet 6 (the Christianity question). The paper reads "Paul warns us" and then
   cites Jude 1:3-4. The book of Jude was written by Jude (the brother of James),
   not by Paul. So the line was set to read "Jude warns us (Jude 1:3-4)". The next
   point, about a different gospel, is genuinely Paul (Galatians 1:6-7), and that
   was left as Paul. Please confirm.


KEPT AS DAD WROTE IT (please review and decide)

2. Sheet 6. For "The truth has once and for all time been revealed", the paper
   cites 2 Peter 1:3. That verse says God's divine power "has given us everything
   we need for a godly life". The exact "once for all entrusted" idea is actually
   in Jude 1:3, which you also quote on the same page. The reference was kept as
   2 Peter 1:3, as written, since it still supports the point. If you meant to
   point only to Jude here, let me know.


NUMBERING (not a fact change)

3. The typed sections ran 1, 2, 3, then jumped to 5, 6, 7, with no section 4. The
   chapter was renumbered 1 to 6 so the sequence is unbroken (the same tidy-up we
   did for the Tithe chapter). The wording of every section is yours. A small
   repeated label, "1) Dreams from God", which only echoed the heading above it,
   was dropped.


A PUBLISHING NOTE (not a correction)

4. This chapter quotes a large number of NIV passages in full (about twenty-seven).
   As noted for the Sabbath chapter, a book that reproduces this much NIV text
   normally needs the standard NIV copyright notice in the front matter, and for
   very large amounts of quoting, written permission from the publisher (Biblica /
   Zondervan). Worth settling before printing. Also, please give the quoted verses
   a final read against a printed NIV; they were taken from an authoritative NIV
   source, but exact wording matters.
