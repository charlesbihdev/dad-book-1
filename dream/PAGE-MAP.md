## Numbering

Dad's typed sections ran 1, 2, 3, then jumped to 5, 6, 7 (no 4). The chapter was
renumbered sequentially 1 to 6 (matching the approach used for the Tithe chapter):

1. Give us your introduction.
2. What is a dream?
3. What do you mean by a dream from God?
4. Explain to us what you mean by natural dreams. (was 5)
5. What are false dreams? (was 6)
6. Does God speak to us in dreams with the coming of Christianity? (was 7)

A stray "1) Dreams from God" label under section 3 (which only repeated the
heading) was dropped as redundant.
