## Numbering

Both sections restart at 1. The characteristics are numbered 1 to 5; "what should
we do" is numbered 1 to 4. The sub-points under a main point (for example the
lifestyle list under characteristic 4, and the "Moses / Simon the sorcerer"
examples under characteristic 2) are rendered as bullets.
