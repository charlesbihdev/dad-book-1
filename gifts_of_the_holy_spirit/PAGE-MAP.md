# Gifts of the Holy Spirit, Page Map (2 photos)

IMPORTANT: this chapter is different from the others. The folder does not contain
Dad's own manuscript. It contains two photographs of a printed REFERENCE BOOK
(an encyclopedia-style article headed "GIFTS FROM GOD", pages 937-938), with
Dad's handwritten topic-label sheets ("GIFTS OF THE HOLY SPIRIT 1" and "GIFTS OF
THE H. SPIRIT 2") laid on top. On Charles's instruction it is treated as an
ordinary source page of the book and transcribed.

The article is prose with scripture CITATIONS only; it does not quote verse text,
so there is no Twi-to-NIV conversion for this chapter.

| Photo | Source file (`...4.50.57 PM`) | Content (one-liner) |
|-------|-------------------------------|---------------------|
| 1 | (no suffix) | "Gifts of the Spirit" intro; the nine gifts listed; "Speech of wisdom"; "Speech of knowledge and faith"; "Healings" (to "...the gift of healing in curing") |
| 2 | `(1)` | continues: Timothy's stomach (1 Tim 5:23); "Powerful works"; "Prophesying"; "Discernment of inspired utterances"; "Tongues" (and its continuation on p. 938); "Interpretation of tongues" |

The two photos are continuous (photo 1 ends mid-sentence "...the gift of healing
in curing" and photo 2 continues "...Timothy of his stomach trouble..."). The
chapter ends naturally at the ninth gift, "Interpretation of tongues"
(1 Corinthians 14:5); the next article subsection begins after that and is not
part of Dad's selection.

## What was transcribed and what was left out

- Started at the article's "Gifts of the Spirit." sub-heading. The dangling tail
  of the previous subsection at the very top of photo 1 ("...any divinely assigned
  service. 1 Timothy 4:14.") was left out, as it is not part of this topic.
- Ended at "Interpretation of tongues" (1 Corinthians 14:5).

## Editorial handling (all noted in the corrections file)

- The abbreviated citations (1Co, Ac, Mt, Joh, Heb, 2Pe, 1Ti, 1Jo, 2Co) were
  expanded to full book names.
- The source puts end-of-sentence citations after an em dash; those were moved
  into the book's parenthetical style so there are no em dashes.
- Two internal cross-references in the source ("see FAITH; HEALING" and "see
  PROPHECY; PROPHET (Prophets in the Christian Greek Scriptures)") were removed,
  because they point to other articles in that encyclopedia, not to anything in
  Dad's book.
- The source's own wording "Jehovah" and its dating style "C.E." were kept
  exactly as printed and flagged for Dad's decision (see corrections).
