SABBATH - POINTS TO REVIEW (FOR DAD)

This note lists every place where a date, number, Bible reference, or fact looked
off while typing the Sabbath chapter. It covers pages 1 to 12 (the handwritten
teaching, Questions 1 to 10). Nothing was changed quietly. Each point says exactly
what was done so Dad can confirm or correct it.


CHANGES MADE (please confirm these are okay)

1. Page 1. The paper says Israel spent "350 years" in Egypt.
   The Bible says 430 years (Exodus 12:40-41). The prophecy says 400 years
   (Genesis 15:13; Acts 7:6). 350 matches neither.
   This was changed to 430 years.

2. Page 10 (the loose slip). The paper says God protested "through the prophet
   Hezekiah" and cites "Hez 20:12-13; 20-21".
   That passage (God gave Israel his Sabbaths and they desecrated them) is in
   Ezekiel 20, not Hezekiah. Hezekiah was a king, not a writing prophet.
   This was changed to: the prophet Ezekiel (Ezekiel 20:12-13, 20-21).


KEPT EXACTLY AS DAD WROTE IT (please review and decide)

3. Page 2. The paper says "the Bible warns us not to think above what is written"
   and cites Romans 12:3.
   The exact phrase "do not go beyond what is written" is 1 Corinthians 4:6.
   Romans 12:3 says "do not think of yourself more highly than you ought." Either
   could be meant.
   Kept as Romans 12:3.

4. Page 4. For the Year of Jubilee the paper cites Leviticus 16:31, then
   Leviticus 25:8-10.
   Leviticus 16:31 is actually the Day of Atonement "sabbath of rest" verse. The
   Jubilee is Leviticus 25:8-10 (already cited). So Leviticus 16:31 may belong to
   the Day of Atonement point just above it.
   Kept at the Jubilee, as written.

5. Page 1. The Hebrew word for Sabbath is written as "Shuvath".
   The usual spelling is "shabbath", from the verb "shavat", meaning to cease or
   rest.
   Kept as "Shuvath".

6. Page 5. The paper says the book of First Corinthians "was written in AD 65".
   Most scholars date 1 Corinthians to about AD 53 to 55 (Paul was likely killed
   around AD 64 to 67, before AD 65 fits well).
   Kept as AD 65.


OTHER THINGS WORTH A LOOK (kept as written)

7. Page 1. Mount Sinai is dated "1513 BC". This follows one chronology; some
   scholars say about 1446 BC. Kept as written.

8. Page 10. The paper says "God swore an oath to cancel the Sabbath" and cites
   Psalm 95:8-11. Psalm 95 actually says God swore "they shall never enter my
   rest"; Hebrews chapters 3 and 4 link that "rest" to the Sabbath-rest, so the
   idea is defensible, but the words "cancel the Sabbath" are Dad's own summary.
   Kept as written.
