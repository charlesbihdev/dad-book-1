TITHE - POINTS TO REVIEW (FOR DAD)

This note lists every place where a Bible reference, a number, or a fact looked
worth a second look while typing the Tithe chapter (the eight handwritten sheets,
Questions 1 to 11). Nothing was changed quietly. Each point says exactly what was
done so Dad can confirm or correct it.


CHANGES MADE (please confirm these are okay)

1. Sheet 2. The paper lists "Josh 13:32-38" for the point that the land was
   divided among the tribes with Levi excluded. Joshua chapter 13 has only 33
   verses, so 13:32-38 does not exist. Verse 33 is the one that makes the point
   ("to the tribe of Levi... the LORD... is their inheritance"). It was set to
   Joshua 13:32-33. Please confirm.


KEPT EXACTLY AS DAD WROTE IT (please review and decide)

2. Sheet 2. In that same list the paper reads "Lev 18:20-26". Leviticus 18:20-26
   is about forbidden sexual relations, not about tithe or land. The passage that
   actually teaches that the Levites received no land and were given the tithe
   instead is Numbers 18:20-26 (the same chapter Dad quotes later for the first
   and second tithes). It looks like Lev may have been written in place of Num.
   It was kept as Leviticus 18:20-26, as Dad wrote it. Please check and tell me
   if it should be Numbers 18:20-26.

3. Sheet 2. The paper says the land was divided "among the eleven tribes of Israel,
   excluding the tribe of Levi". Counting Levi out of the twelve tribes leaves
   eleven, so this reads correctly; just note that the land was in fact shared in
   twelve portions because Joseph's share was split between Ephraim and Manasseh.
   Kept exactly as written.

4. Sheet 1. One of the opening facts says "money not properly acquired is the root
   cause of all evil." The familiar verse (1 Timothy 6:10) says "the love of money
   is a root of all kinds of evil." No verse was cited here on the paper, so
   nothing was added. Kept exactly as Dad wrote it.


THINGS CHECKED THAT ARE FINE (no change, just so you know)

5. Sheet 3, Question 4. The paper cites Genesis 17:12 to show money existed in
   Abraham's time. Genesis 17 is mainly about circumcision, but verse 12 does
   mention servants "bought with money", so the reference does support the point.
   Kept as written.

6. Sheet 3. Mark 12:41 (Jesus watching people put their offerings into the temple
   treasury) was checked and fits the point about giving a collection. Kept.

7. Sheet 8. Acts 4:36-37 was checked: the Levite who sold his field and brought the
   money to the apostles is Barnabas (Joseph the Levite), so the reference fits the
   point exactly. Kept.


A NOTE ON NUMBERING AND ORDER (not a fact change)

8. On the last three sheets the question numbers and the sheet numbers did not line
   up. "How many types of tithes" was numbered 6, and "How does the church do
   projects" was also numbered 6; "did Jesus/apostles collect" and "what problems
   does tithe pose" sat on swapped sheets (the content shows the order is projects,
   then did-Jesus, then problems). The two sections "Why Malachi 3:10 cannot be
   used" and "Why Matthew 23:23 can't be justified" had no numbers. As agreed, the
   chapter was renumbered 1 to 11 in reading order, with those two sections made
   into their own numbered questions. The wording of every section is Dad's own.
