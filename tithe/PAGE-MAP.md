## Reading order and numbering

Dad's question numbers and his sheet numbers do not line up on the last three sheets:
- "How many types of tithes" is numbered 6, but "How does the church do projects" is ALSO numbered 6.
- "Did Jesus/apostles collect" (his 7) and "What problems..." (his 8) sit on physically swapped sheets. Content proves the true order is projects, then did-Jesus, then problems: sheet 8 opens by continuing the projects point (Exodus 36:4-7), and sheet 7 opens with a crossed-out false start of the Acts 4:36-37 line that actually ends sheet 8.
- "Why Malachi 3:10 cannot be used" and "Why Matthew 23:23 can't be justified" had no numbers.

Per Charles's decision, the chapter is renumbered 1 to 11 in content order, with the Malachi and Matthew sections becoming their own numbered questions:

1. What is tithing?
2. Give a brief history about tithing.
3. What were the things used to pay tithe?
4. Maybe, during Bible time, there was no money.
5. Why members of the Churches of Christ do not pay tithe.
6. How many types of tithes did God order the Jews to observe?
7. Why Malachi 3:10 cannot be used by Christians to pay tithe.
8. Why Matthew 23:23 cannot be justified to pay tithe.
9. How does the Church do projects without collecting tithes?
10. Did Jesus or the apostles collect, receive, or instruct Christians to pay tithe?
11. What problems do payment of tithe pose to Christianity?
