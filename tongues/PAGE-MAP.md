## Structure

- Questions 1 to 13 are numbered on the paper.
- After Q13 a second numbered list starts again at 1: "Is it like Acts 2?",
  "...Acts 10?", "...Acts 19?", "...1 Cor 12-14 today?" (numbers 2 and 3 are
  faint on sheet 3). The draft keeps that restart and gives each "Is it like..."
  question as a sub-heading. No heading was added above it.
